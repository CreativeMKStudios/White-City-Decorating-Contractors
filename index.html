What Simon does

The work is painting and decorating for houses. He will tell you if a surface needs more prep, and he will say which paint suits the job.

  • Interior painting Walls, ceilings and woodwork, including new plaster.
  • Kitchen spraying Units sprayed a new colour, with a smooth finish.
  • Woodwork and stairs Doors, banisters, spindles and skirting, prepared properly.
  • Wallpapering Paper hung straight, including awkward rooms.
  • Exterior painting Outside doors, sheds and timber, when the weather allows.

Questions people ask

Who does the painting?

Simon Hewitt. He does the work himself, from the first look at the room to the last coat.

Where does he work?

He is based in Sandy. Most jobs are in Sandy, Biggleswade, Potton, Bedford and St Neots. The areas page lists the other towns.

Can he spray a kitchen?

Yes. He sprays unit doors, drawers and frames in a new colour. He also sprays internal doors and made-to-measure wardrobes.

Does he paint new plaster?

Yes. He paints newly plastered ceilings and walls, and he prepares the surface before the colour goes on.

How do I get a price?

Call 07903 197206 or email simon-hewitt@hotmail.co.uk. Say which rooms need doing, and Simon will arrange a time to look.
