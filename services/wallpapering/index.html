Wallpapering

Simon hangs wallpaper as well as paint. That includes rooms where the walls are uneven or the paper is hard to match. He also strips old paper when that is the right start.

Rob Shirley asked Simon to redecorate a whole house after an extension. The job included new plaster, oak doors, and a lot of difficult wallpaper. He wrote that the finish was right in every room, and he called Simon back years later.

Wallpaper and paint often share a room. Simon will say which walls should be papered and which should be painted, and he will line up the pattern so the joins do not jump out.

Where this work is done
